## 1. Executive Summary & Market Positioning

This proposal establishes the complete **Shopify App Store Listing Metadata** and **Freemium Tier Architecture** for our Size Chart plugin. 

Based on competitive benchmarking against category leaders (*Avada Size Chart* and *MP Size Chart & Size Guide*), our core strategy is:
1. **Acquire & Delight Free Merchants:** Provide a generous, watermark-free free plan to win early market share, drive organic installs, and accumulate 5-star merchant reviews.
2. **Monetize Growing & Enterprise Catalogs:** Introduce progressive paid tiers (**$6.95**, **$12.95**, and **$24.95/month**) targeting stores that expand past 2 size charts, require tag/vendor targeting, need custom CSS, or desire engagement analytics.
3. **No Code Pollution:** Leverage our 100% Theme App Extension foundation as a key marketing differentiator — zero script clutter, instant load times, and effortless 1-click theme customizer setup.

## 4. Feature Gating Analysis (What to Save for Paid Tiers)

To balance rapid merchant adoption with high conversion to paid plans:

### What Remains in the Free Tier (Starter):
* **2 Active Size Charts:** Sufficient for boutique stores to cover their hero products and leave positive reviews.
* **Popup Modal & Inline Embed:** The two most requested display styles.
* **Product & Collection Targeting:** Covers the standard catalog structure of small merchants.
* **Inches / CM Unit Conversion:** A core differentiator vs. competitors who restrict conversion to paid plans.
* **No Branding / Watermarks:** Retaining watermark-free storefronts builds merchant trust and loyalty.

### What Gets Gated for Paid Tiers (Growth & Pro):
1. **Chart Capacity (3 to Unlimited):** Natural trigger for catalogs expanding past 2 charts.
2. **Advanced Display Modes:** Floating Edge Button and Collapsible Accordion reserved for Growth ($6.95).
3. **Tag, Vendor & Product Type Targeting:** Essential for multi-brand or drop-shipping stores with hundreds of SKUs.
4. **Raw Custom CSS Injection:** Advanced merchants and agencies requiring custom styling upgrade to Pro ($12.95).
5. **Chart Duplication:** High-efficiency cloning tools reserved for Pro.
6. **Analytics & Click Tracking:** Clear ROI visibility for Pro and Advanced merchants.

## 5. Technical Implementation Roadmap (Post-Approval)

Once team sign-off is confirmed:

1. **Phase 1: Shopify Billing API**
   * Configure recurring subscriptions via GraphQL `appSubscriptionCreate` mutation.
   * Support 14-day free trial and billing status webhooks (`app_subscriptions/update`).
2. **Phase 2: Database Plan Modeling**
   * Update Prisma `Shop` model with `plan String @default("free")`, `chargeId String?`, `trialEndsAt DateTime?`.
3. **Phase 3: Admin Soft-Gating UI**
   * Implement non-intrusive Polaris upgrade modal triggered when free merchants attempt to activate a 3rd chart or select paid targeting/display options.
4. **Phase 4: App Store Assets & Review Preparation**
   * Design 1200x1200px App Icon, 1600x900px promotional gallery images, and finalize Privacy Policy & Terms of Service URLs.
